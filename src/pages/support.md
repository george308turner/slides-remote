# Slides Remote Support

Need a hand with Slides Remote? Pick the guide for the remote you use. Each one covers getting started, pairing, notes, timers, the unlock and troubleshooting.

- **[iPhone and Apple Watch, with a Mac]({{URL:support/apple/}})**: the help guide for the iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac apps.
- **[Android phone and Wear OS watch, with a Mac or Windows PC]({{URL:support/android/}})**: the help guide for the Android and Wear OS apps, including setting up Slides Remote for Windows.

## Contact

Questions not answered in the guides, problems and suggestions: {{EMAIL}}. Please say which devices you use (for example "iPhone 17 and a MacBook Pro with macOS 26") and what you were doing when the problem happened.

## Purchases and refunds

The Notes & Timers unlock is bought through your device's app store, which also handles refunds:

- **App Store (Mac, iPhone, Apple Watch):** [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)
- **Google Play (Android, Wear OS):** [Google Play's refund help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637)
- **Microsoft Store (Windows):** [Microsoft's refund help](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accounts-billing/subscriptions/get-a-refund-for-apps-and-games-purchased-from-microsoft-store)

To get a purchase back after reinstalling or on a new device, use **Restore Purchases** in the app.
